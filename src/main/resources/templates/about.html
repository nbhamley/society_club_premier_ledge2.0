About SCPL

Society Club Premier League is one platform for many residential societies. Each society gets its own season, teams, players, auction, fixtures and admins, completely separate from every other society.

Private by design

Player contact details are visible only to the player and their own society's admins. Public pages show names, roles and statistics. Platform staff never see player details.

Decisions need agreement

Approving players, dropping players, deleting a season and bulk imports each need votes from several of your admins, so no single admin can change things alone.

Your data is yours

Export an encrypted backup of everything at any time, even if a subscription lapses.
