Backups

Your society is read-only, but you can still create and download backups of your data.

A backup is a single encrypted file (.scplenc) with all of your society's data. Only SCPL can open it: a platform admin restores it into a new society. It does not include logins or payment details. Backups are kept for 30 days.

Up to three per day.
